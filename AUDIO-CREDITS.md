# Audio credits

## Supplementary classroom recordings

The 19 supplementary Mandarin syllable recordings used to complete the 35-final inventory come from
[Tone Perfect: Multimodal Database for Mandarin Chinese](https://tone.lib.msu.edu/), developed by
Catherine Ryu, the Mandarin Tone Perception & Production Team, and Michigan State University Libraries.

- Speaker: Female Voice 1 (FV1), a native Mandarin speaker
- Files used: individual 320 kbit/s MP3 teaching recordings
- Rights statement: In Copyright — Educational Use Permitted
- Use in this project: local, non-commercial classroom instruction

The recordings model each final inside a pronounceable syllable where an isolated final is not a
valid Mandarin syllable. Most use tone 1; `uo` uses `wǒ` and `er` uses `ér`.

The isolated `ong` teaching recording is from the pinyin finals resource at
[One-To-One Chinese Lessons](https://www.121chineselessons.com/resources/pinyin/finals/). It is used
in this local classroom copy as an isolated-final demonstration rather than as a lexical syllable.
