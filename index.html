Finals · stage five

Finish in back: –ng.

The back of the tongue rises; the tongue tip stays relaxed. Compare each –n / –ng pair.

Feel it: an ↔ ang · en ↔ eng · in ↔ ing. The –ng finals are not simply “–n plus g.”

10 / 15

Special final

er stands alone.

Unlike the other finals, er does not combine with an initial. The tongue tip curls back while the vowel continues.

1 · Start with the vowelBegin with a relaxed central vowel—not English “ee.”
2 · Curl, do not addRaise the tongue tip back gradually. Do not pronounce a separate English r.
3 · Keep the voice continuousThe vowel and rhotic quality form one sound.

Important: this standalone final is different from the –r suffix added in érhuà.
